# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the user: React with a small Vinext backend and D1 database for shared rooms.
## Users
South Asian students, parents, and facilitators, using individual phones and optionally a shared room display.
## Product Purpose
Lower the social stakes of practicing honest conversation, challenging assumptions, and understanding another generation.
## Positioning
Anonymous cross-generational responses are a guessing and prediction game, followed by reflection and a consent-based shared letter.
## Operating Context
A host opens a room; players join with a code, choose student or parent, and complete a personalization quiz. A solo practice mode uses clearly labeled example responses.
## Capabilities and Constraints
User confirmed a playable multiplayer app plus solo practice. Guess Who Said It, Prediction Markets, Scenario Triage, and Joint Build derive from the supplied Setu proposal. No names or family pairing. Mental health topics require opt-in. Players can skip. Research informs the concept; no claims of validated mental health outcomes.
Each home game card opens its own launcher. Hosts can choose all three games or a single game; solo practice follows the same selection. Quick sessions use one round per selected game; full sessions use its available scenarios.
## Brand Commitments
Setu. Welcoming, respectful, accessible language without clinical framing or stereotypes.
## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied Google Doc 1UpNhdBzdeYLYzegwf3E5wd3IAOypIcU4EqEv6PxKeOY. Scenario text and mechanics come from this proposal. No product outcome evidence or testimonials.
## Product Principles
- Separate players' anonymous answers from example content.
- Reward understanding without treating disagreement as failure.
- Make every sensitive disclosure optional.
- Ask permission before retaining a response in the closing artifact.
## Open Decisions
Assumed first-release scope: facilitator advances phases, room expires after 24 hours, closing letter link expires with room. No longitudinal participant accounts.
